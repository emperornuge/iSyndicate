# App Store listing draft

## Name (30 chars max)
iSyndicate

## Subtitle (30 chars max)
Build your crime empire

## Description
Build your criminal empire from the streets up. Run missions, stack cash,
gear up with weapons and armor, buy real estate for passive income, and grow
your mob by recruiting other players with your invite code.

• 8 story missions across 3 syndicate tiers
• PvP fight arena — rob rival mobsters for their unbanked cash
• Equipment shop: weapons, armor, getaway vehicles
• Real-estate empire with hourly payouts and item upkeep
• Mob system: recruit players, multiply your battle power
• Bank vault with deposit protection, underground hospital

Your energy, stamina, and health regenerate over time — come back often,
stay dangerous.

## Age rating
Expect 17+ — frequent/intense cartoon or fantasy violence, criminal themes.
(Apple assigns the final rating from the questionnaire; answer honestly.)

## Privacy
- Data collected: user alias (player-chosen username), linked to the user,
  used for app functionality only. No tracking, no third-party SDKs.
- Privacy manifest is bundled (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy). The nutrition label in
  App Store Connect must mirror it: "User ID — linked to user — app functionality."

TestFlight build against our live game server. Seeded AI rivals are available
in the Fight tab from first launch."
